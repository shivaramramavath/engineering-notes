# NestJS Interview: Defending System Design Decisions

### Strategic Defenses:
- **Why NestJS over Go / Java?**: TypeScript end-to-end type safety with frontend teams, massive npm ecosystem, rapid prototyping velocity with enterprise IoC discipline.
- **Why Modular Monolith before Microservices?**: Eliminates network serialization overhead, distributed transactions, and multi-repo operational friction until team scale demands decoupling.
