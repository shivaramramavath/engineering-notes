# Module 19 Overview: How Design Interviews Are Graded

Interviewers rarely want textbook definitions. Strong answers follow **Problem → Trade-off → Pattern → Alternative**:
1. *"What breaks without it?"* (e.g., `Square extends Rectangle` breaks caller expectations → LSP).
2. *"What are the trade-offs?"* (every abstraction adds indirection).
3. *"When would you NOT use it?"* (shows senior judgment, not pattern memorization).
