# Behavioural & Project Design Discussion

How to talk about architecture and patterns from your own projects using **Context → Problem → Options → Decision → Outcome**:

1. **Describe a time you refactored messy code**: Mention *Characterisation Tests*, *Extract Class / Guard Clauses*, and measurable impact (fewer bugs, faster tests).
2. **Describe a time you chose simplicity over a complex pattern**: Mention *YAGNI / Modular Monolith* over premature Microservices, or a *pure function* over a 4-class Strategy hierarchy.
3. **Describe a production reliability incident you prevented or fixed**: Mention *Timeouts, Circuit Breaker, Idempotency Key, Bounded Queues (Backpressure)*, or *Transactional Outbox*.
