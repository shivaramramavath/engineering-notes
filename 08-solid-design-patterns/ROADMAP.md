# Software Design & Patterns Learning Roadmap (ROADMAP.md)

This roadmap outlines structured, goal-oriented learning tracks for engineers at different career stages.

---

```
[Path A: Foundations & SOLID (Junior / Mid)]
  01-foundations -> 02-solid-principles -> 03-design-principles -> 04-patterns-intro
       |
       v
[Path B: GoF Classics (Mid / Senior)]

       |
```

---

## 2. Track Profiles & Time Estimates

### Track 1: Junior Engineer to Mid-Level (Estimated: 25–35 Hours)
- **Goal:** Master core OOP foundations, write maintainable code, stop creating God Objects and violating LSP.
- **Key Modules:**
  1. `01-foundations`: Encapsulation, Composition vs Inheritance, Coupling/Cohesion, Programming to Interfaces.
  2. `02-solid-principles`: Master all 5 SOLID principles with realistic failure scenarios.
  3. `03-design-principles`: DRY, KISS, YAGNI, Law of Demeter, Tell-Don't-Ask.
  4. `04-patterns-intro` & `05-creational-patterns`: Factory Method, Builder, Singleton trade-offs.

### Track 2: Senior Backend Engineer (Estimated: 40–50 Hours)
- **Goal:** Design scalable, modular backend services; decouple domain logic from infrastructure; master concurrency and resilience.
- **Key Modules:**
  1. `06-structural-patterns` & `07-behavioral-patterns`: Decorator, Adapter, Strategy, Observer, Command, Chain of Responsibility.
  2. `08-concurrency-patterns`: Thread Pool, Producer-Consumer, Read-Write Lock, Immutability.
  3. `09-enterprise-application-patterns`: Repository, Unit of Work, Service Layer, DTO, Domain Events.
  4. `11-distributed-and-cloud-patterns`: Circuit Breaker, Retry/Jitter, Saga, Transactional Outbox.

### Track 3: Software Architect & Tech Lead (Estimated: 35–45 Hours)
- **Goal:** Drive strategic and tactical architecture; manage evolutionary monoliths vs microservices; evaluate anti-patterns; establish design review standards.
- **Key Modules:**
  1. `10-architecture`: Clean/Hexagonal/Onion, Modular Monolith, DDD, Event-Driven, ADRs.
  2. `13-anti-patterns-and-code-smells`: God Object, Anemic Domain, Primitive Obsession, Shotgun Surgery.
  3. `14-refactoring` & `16-production`: Strangler Fig, Branch by Abstraction, Maintainability, Security by Design.
  4. `17-case-studies`: Review end-to-end multi-pattern architectures.

### Track 4: Technical Interview Preparation (Estimated: 15–20 Hours)
- **Goal:** Ace Low-Level Design (LLD), Object-Oriented Design (OOD), and System Design interviews.
- **Key Modules:**
  1. `18-pattern-selection`: Decision trees and comparison matrices.
  2. `19-interview`: 10 comprehensive interview guides with output questions and behavioral rubrics.
  3. `20-cheatsheets`: One-page recaps, GoF at a glance, and UML recipes.
  4. `17-case-studies`: Parking Lot, Elevator Controller, Notification Engine, Payment Gateway.
